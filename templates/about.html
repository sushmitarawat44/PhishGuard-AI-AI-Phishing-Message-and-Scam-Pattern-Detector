About PhishGuard AI

An educational phishing-message screening prototype built with Python, NLP, and a web interface.

How it works

  1. Text is accepted from the user.
  2. TF-IDF converts text into numerical features.
  3. A Logistic Regression classifier estimates similarity to the bundled labelled examples.
  4. Transparent rules look for suspicious requests, urgency, and links.
  5. The application combines signals into an illustrative risk indicator and shows warnings.

Limitations

  • The bundled dataset is small and illustrative, not representative of real-world traffic.
  • The displayed risk score is a heuristic, not a calibrated probability.
  • The system does not inspect link destinations or sender authentication.
  • It cannot guarantee a message is safe or malicious.
